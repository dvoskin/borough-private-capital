Services

Property Management Services

One accountable management relationship for the daily work that protects income, tenant experience, and the physical asset. Scope and reporting are built around the property.

Request a Proposal

Services by Property Type

Scope is built around the property: residential and mixed use buildings, commercial properties, and the operating, financial, compliance, and capital work each one requires.

Multifamily & Mixed Use

Rental buildings, apartment portfolios, and buildings with residential units above retail or office space.

  • Rent collection, arrears tracking, and tenant communication
  • Vacancy marketing, leasing coordination, renewals, and turnovers
  • Superintendent, cleaning, waste, pest control, and vendor oversight
  • Maintenance requests, inspections, and building system upkeep

Commercial Properties

Retail storefronts, office buildings, and commercial spaces with lease specific operating requirements.

  • Lease administration, critical dates, and tenant coordination
  • Common area maintenance budgets and reconciliations
  • Expense recoveries, utility allocations, and vendor contracts
  • Tenant improvements, build outs, and capital project coordination
Financial Management & Owner Reporting

A consistent picture of income, expenses, and outstanding issues.

  • Rent rolls, collections, arrears, and accounts payable
  • Operating budgets, cash flow tracking, and reserve planning
  • Monthly statements, bank reconciliation, and invoice records
  • Year end document preparation for your accountant
Maintenance & Building Operations

Routine upkeep and a defined escalation process for urgent issues.

  • Work order intake, vendor dispatch, and repair follow through
  • Preventive maintenance for heating, ventilation, air conditioning, plumbing, and building systems
  • Cleaning, landscaping, snow removal, waste, and pest services
  • After hours response arrangements, access control, and site staffing
Leasing & Tenant Relations
  • Listing and showing coordination with appropriate leasing professionals
  • Application processing and screening under consistent criteria
  • Lease execution support, renewals, move ins, and move outs
  • Tenant requests, notices, and documentation; counsel coordination for disputes
Compliance & Risk Coordination
  • Property specific registration, notice, and inspection calendars
  • Inspection coordination with New York City housing and buildings agencies, the New Jersey Department of Community Affairs, or municipal inspectors, as applicable
  • Violation tracking and remediation with qualified professionals
  • Vendor insurance records, incident documentation, and claims support
  • Coordination of applicable fire, elevator, boiler, facade, and energy requirements
Capital Improvements & Procurement
  • Property condition reviews and repair priority planning
  • Bid collection, scope comparison, and owner approval
  • Contractor scheduling, progress tracking, and invoice review
  • Renovation and tenant improvement coordination through closeout
Commercial Lease & Expense Administration
  • Lease abstracts, rent escalations, options, and expiration tracking
  • Common area maintenance and operating expense reconciliations under each lease
  • Tax and insurance pass through tracking where applicable
  • Tenant insurance certificates, maintenance obligations, and service coordination

Management scope, fees, spending authority, reporting cadence, and emergency coverage are defined in the agreement. Legal, brokerage, engineering, and other regulated work is handled by appropriately licensed professionals where required.
