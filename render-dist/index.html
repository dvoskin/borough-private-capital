Who is this for?

Mortgage brokers, real estate brokers, agents, sponsors, and investors seeking business-purpose investment-property financing.

What should I include with a deal?

Property location, requested loan amount, financing purpose, target closing date, and a short note on value, budget, and exit.

Are these approvals or rate quotes?

No. An inquiry starts a conversation; pricing, leverage, fees, and timing depend on underwriting and documentation.
