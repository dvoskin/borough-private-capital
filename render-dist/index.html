Frequently Asked Questions

What Types of Properties Do You Finance?

Business purpose investment properties, including multifamily, mixed use, residential investment, and commercial property. Owner occupied home loans are not offered.

Which Markets Do You Lend In?

New York City, Northern New Jersey, and Metro Atlanta.

What Should the First Request Include?

Property location and asset type, purchase price or current basis, requested loan amount and use of proceeds, and the business plan, timing, and exit strategy.

The Borrower Guide lists the documents lenders commonly request later in underwriting.

Is an Inquiry an Approval?

No. Loan availability, pricing, fees, leverage, and timing depend on underwriting, eligibility, documentation, and funding availability. An inquiry is not an approval or commitment to lend.

See what can change between a quote and closing.

Can Brokers Submit Deals?

Yes. State your role and communication preference with the first brief. The Broker Partners page explains what to include.

Should I Send Bank Statements or Identity Documents?

Not with the initial brief. Keep bank statements, identity documents, and confidential borrower records out of the website brief.
