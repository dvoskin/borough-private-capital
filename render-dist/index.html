Questions

Before you begin.

Can I use management without taking a loan?

Yes. Property management and financing are separate service discussions. Your management needs can be reviewed independently of any financing request.

What does a management proposal include?

A proposed scope, fees, reporting schedule, spending authority, emergency arrangements, and transition requirements. The exact scope depends on the property, lease obligations, and existing staff or vendors.

Can you coordinate a change from my current manager?

A transition scope can include leases, rent rolls, financial records, vendor agreements, keys and access, open work orders, and tenant communications. Timing depends on the current agreement and record availability.

How are commercial properties different?

Commercial management adds lease-specific items such as expense recoveries, CAM reconciliations, tenant improvement coordination, rent escalations, and critical dates. Responsibilities are reviewed against each lease.

Does management replace the owner's legal obligations?

No. The agreement defines delegated responsibilities. Owners retain applicable obligations, and legal or technical matters require the appropriate qualified professionals.
