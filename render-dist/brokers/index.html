A cleaner broker submission path.

Send enough information for a useful first review while avoiding sensitive documents until the request has a defined next step.

01

Share the snapshot

Property, basis or purchase price, requested proceeds, use of funds, timing, and exit.

02

Set communication

Identify your role, client relationship, preferred contact method, and how you want updates handled.

03

Advance the file

Clarify fit, outstanding information, decision points, and responsibility for the next step.

Ideal first submission
  • Property address and asset type
  • Purchase price or current basis
  • Requested loan amount and purpose
  • Renovation scope if applicable
  • Exit strategy and closing target
Your relationship

State whether you are representing the borrower, sourcing the property, or coordinating the financing, and specify your communication preference.

What happens next

The initial review should identify whether the request may fit, what information remains necessary, and whether a deeper underwriting conversation is appropriate.

Sensitive information

Keep bank statements, identity documents, and confidential borrower records out of the initial website brief.
