The initial review is designed to establish fit and identify the property information needed for a financing conversation.

03

Rental property

Financing conversations for income-producing investment properties and portfolio opportunities.

What to include
  • Property location and asset type
  • Purchase price or current basis
  • Requested loan amount and use of proceeds
  • Business plan, timing, and exit strategy
Acquisition requests

Include the contract status, target closing date, planned equity contribution, property condition, and immediate work required.

Refinance requests

Include current debt, payoff needs, ownership history, property performance, requested cash-out if applicable, and the intended exit.

Underwriting conversation

Pricing, leverage, fees, documentation, and timing depend on the property, borrower, business plan, eligibility, and funding availability.
