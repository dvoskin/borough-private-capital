Lending Programs

The initial review is designed to establish fit and identify the property information needed for a financing conversation.

Bridge Financing

Acquisition, refinance, or transitional financing ahead of a sale, stabilization, or longer term capital.

Fix & Flip

Purchase or refinance tied to a renovation scope, project budget, timeline, and resale or refinance strategy.

Rental Property

Financing conversations for income producing investment properties and portfolio opportunities.

What to Include
  • Property location and asset type
  • Purchase price or current basis
  • Requested loan amount and use of proceeds
  • Business plan, timing, and exit strategy
Acquisition Requests

Include the contract status, target closing date, planned equity contribution, property condition, and immediate work required.

Refinance Requests

Include current debt, payoff needs, ownership history, property performance, requested cash out if applicable, and the intended exit.

Underwriting Conversation

Pricing, leverage, fees, documentation, and timing depend on the property, borrower, business plan, eligibility, and funding availability.

Our Lending Process

A useful financing process gives brokers and investors a direct read on fit, missing information, and the next decision.

Property Led Review

The conversation starts with the actual asset and plan rather than a generic loan label.

Defined Next Step

Understand what information is required and what must be resolved before moving forward.

Broker Aware Communication

Establish the broker’s role and communication preference at the beginning of the request.

Prepare a Deal Brief

Do not include banking, identity, or sensitive borrower documents in the initial brief. An inquiry is not an approval or commitment to lend.
